    Good to know

    Do I need to pay online?

    No. You pay at the clinic after your consultation by cash, card or UPI.

    Can I reschedule?

    Yes, reply to your WhatsApp confirmation with a new time and we'll update it.

    Is there parking?

    Two-wheeler parking is available in front of the clinic.
